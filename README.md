## 📌 Overview

**WealthWing** is an AI-powered financial inclusion platform designed to help **credit-invisible individuals** and **first-time investors** gain access to transparent credit scoring and personalized wealth-building guidance.

Traditional financial institutions rely heavily on formal credit history, leaving millions of Indians unable to access loans or financial products. WealthWing bridges this gap by using **alternative digital signals** such as utility payments, mobile recharges, and e-commerce activity to estimate creditworthiness while providing explainable AI insights.

In addition, WealthWing helps users begin their investment journey through a conversational financial advisor that recommends simple micro-investment strategies tailored to individual risk profiles.

---

# 🚨 Problem Statement

More than **190 million adults in India** remain credit invisible due to the absence of traditional credit history.

These users often experience:

- ❌ Loan rejections without explanations
- ❌ No formal credit score
- ❌ Limited financial literacy
- ❌ Confusing investment products
- ❌ No personalized investment guidance

The TetraTHON FinTech challenge required participants to build a system that:

- Generates transparent credit scores using non-traditional digital signals
- Explains why a user received a score
- Suggests actionable steps for improving it
- Recommends beginner-friendly investment options through conversational AI
- Provides educational projections rather than financial advice

---

# 💡 Our Solution

WealthWing combines **Alternative Credit Scoring** with **AI-powered Financial Coaching** inside one unified platform.

Instead of depending on salary slips or bank history, our system learns from responsible digital behavior.

Examples include:

- Electricity bill payments
- LPG bill payments
- Mobile recharge frequency
- E-commerce activity
- UPI transaction consistency
- Digital payment habits

These signals are transformed into an explainable credit score using Machine Learning.

Users also receive:

- Credit improvement roadmap
- Risk profiling chatbot
- Personalized micro-investment recommendations
- Goal-based savings lockers
- Financial education in regional languages

# 🔄 Workflow

1. User registers securely.
2. User provides consent.
3. Alternative financial data is collected.
4. ML model predicts credit score.
5. SHAP explains important features.
6. AI chatbot profiles investment risk.
7. Personalized recommendations are generated.
8. User tracks financial growth over time.
